Backend Software Engineer

We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer with strong experience in
Python to join our growing engineering team. You will design and build
scalable microservices, work with distributed systems, and collaborate
closely with product and data teams.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of professional experience in Python development
- Experience with Django or Flask
- Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL or other relational databases
- Experience with AWS or another cloud platform
- Familiarity with Docker and containerized deployments
- Experience building and maintaining REST APIs
- Bonus: experience with Pandas or NumPy for data processing
- Bonus: experience with Kubernetes and CI/CD pipelines

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain backend services and APIs
- Optimize systems for performance and reliability at scale
- Write clean, well-tested, maintainable Python code
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams on technical design
- Participate in code review and mentor junior engineers
